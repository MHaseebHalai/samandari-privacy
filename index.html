Privacy policy

Samandari, fresh sea fish delivered in Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Last updated 24 November 2026.

What we collect

When you message Samandari on WhatsApp or Instagram, we receive your name as shown on your account, your phone number or handle, and the messages you send. To deliver an order we ask for your delivery address, a location pin and a payment screenshot.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and take your order.
  • To arrange delivery and confirm payment.
  • To tell you about our next delivery or an offer. You can ask us to stop at any time.

An automated assistant answers first messages on WhatsApp so you get a quick reply. Payment, complaints and anything unusual are passed to a person on our team.

Who sees it

Our team and the rider delivering your order (name, address and phone only). Messages are handled through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, and the assistant's replies are written using Anthropic's Claude. We do not sell or share your details with anyone else.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for our accounts. You can ask us to delete your chat history and details at any time.

Contact

To ask a question, see what we hold or have it deleted, message us on WhatsApp at 0329 506 0254.
